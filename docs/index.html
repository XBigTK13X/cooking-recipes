Biscuits

Ingredients

  • 313 grams ap flour
  • 2 tablespoons alumiunum free power of baking
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 113 grams unsalted butter
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tablespoons buttermilk
  • 14 grams honey

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425
  2. Mix the ap flour, salt, and powder of baking
  3. Cut butter into the dry mix until coarse crumbs
  4. Make a well, drizzle the honey and 1 cup of buttermilk into it
  5. Barely fold the wet into the dry
  6. Dump out the dough and gently bring together with extra flour
  7. Flatten with your hands into 3/4 inch high rectangle, fold bottom 1/3 to the middle and other side to the opposite edge.
  8. Turn 90 degrees and repeat 3 more times flattening and folding.
  9. Cut straight down into rounds and bake for 20 minutes or until tops are browned

Notes

I've tried a lot of biscuit receipes. This is the only good one.

The colder the dough and butter, the better

Cyclone

Ingredients

  • 200 grams premium frozen custard (i.e. Costco)
  • 25 grams mixins (see mods)
  • 40 grams premium choc chips
  • 5 grams vegetable oil
  • 5 grams heavy cream
  • 5 grams corn syrup

Steps

  1. Microwave the chocolate chips, oil, and cream for 20 seconds
  2. Aggresively fold the corn syrup into the hot chocolate mix
  3. Allow the fudge drizzle to cool
  4. Spread the ice cream over a flat surface, like a large cutting board
  5. Sprinkle toppings and drizzle fudge over the ice cream
  6. Use an ice cream spade and bench scraper to quickly mix it all together
  7. Chuck the mix into two small bowls and freeze them for at least 15 minutes

Mods

These ingredients are per bowl (so double for a batch in the recipe)

Drumstick -> 25g fudge, 15g unsalted roasted peanuts, 8g waffle cone or 15g animal cracker

Peanut Butter Cup -> 2 Reeses eggs per bowl

Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • 2 pounds sweet italian sausage
  • 1 teapsoon red pepper flake
  • 4 jars Rao's Marinara
  • 2 tubs ricota
  • 1 pound mozzarella (cubed)
  • 1 cup parm (grated)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 tube basil paste
  • 4 cloves garlic (smashed)
  • 2 boxes Lasagna noodles

Steps

  1. Cook the beef and sausage together with red pepper flake
  2. Stir 3 of the marinara jars into the meat sauce, then leave alone
  3. In a large bowl mix the ricotta, mozzarella, parm, garlic, basil paste, and eggs to form the cheese sauce
  4. Either cook off lasagna noodles (better) or be lazy with oven-ready noodles (comes out almost as good)
  5. For each of the 4 trays, do a layer of meat sauce, then noodle, then cheese, then noodle, then meat, back and forth until finally meat on top
  6. For each tray (esp. if using oven ready noodles) pour marinara over the top so the sides aren't exposed raw noodle
  7. Throw in the freezer uncooked (use a deodorizer, it will stink up the freezer)
  8. When time to cook, hit the oven to 425 and bake covered for one hour
  9. Once internal temp hits at least 165, remove the cover and sprinkle pepperoni and mushrooms on top.
  10. Broil until the pepperoni is crispy.

Mug Cake

Ingredients

  • 30 grams hot cocoa mix
  • 50 grams ap flour
  • 5 grams aluminum free powder of baking
  • 1 pinch fine salt
  • 20 grams white sugar
  • 60 grams whole milk
  • 30 grams veg oil or olive oil
  • 1 dollop nutella

Steps

  1. Gently mix the ap flour, cocoa mix, powder of baking, and salt until no lumps in a large coffee mug
  2. Use a small spatula to barely stir the oil, sugar, and milk into the dry until incorporated
  3. Push the nutella into the center of the batter
  4. Microwave for 1 minute per mug

Apple Crisp

Ingredients

  • 4 apples (thinly sliced)
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup ap flour
  • 1/3 cup butter (softened)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350
  2. Grease an 8x8x2 inch pan
  3. Spread apple slices evenly throughout pan
  4. Mix water, cinnamon, and salt in a bowl
  5. Pour cinnamon mixture over apples
  6. Mix sugar, flour, and butter in a bowl
  7. Spread crumb mixture over the apples
  8. Bake for 40 minutes or until crumb is golden brown

Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 113 grams unsalted butter (melted)
  • 2 egg
  • 340 grams banana (overripe)
  • 100 grams sour cream
  • 150 grams brown sugar
  • 60 grams apricot jam or orange marmalade
  • 80 grams honey
  • 3 grams (1 teaspoon) cinnamon
  • 1 grams (1/4 teaspoon) nutmeg
  • 3 grams (1 teaspoon) soda of baking
  • 3 grams (1 teaspoon) powder of baking
  • 3 grams (1 teaspoon) table salt
  • 3 grams (1 teaspoon) vanilla extract
  • 275 grams ap flour
  • 60 grams pecans (chopped)
  • 100 grams chocolate (chopped)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees
  2. Spray a cake round with cooking spray, cover the bottom with parchment, spray the parchment
  3. Melt the butter in the microwave in a large mixing bowl
  4. Mash the bananas into the melted butter using a potato masher
  5. Stir in everything except the ap flour, chocolate, and pecans
  6. Once well mixed, gently fold in the ap flour, chocolate, and pecans
  7. Pour into a cake round and bake for ~1 hour, or until the cake is completely set and springs back when patted (~210 F). If the top is getting too dark around ~45 minutes, then tent with foil.
  8. Rest for half an hour in the round, then remove to a cooling rack to cool completely.
  9. Eat immediately, or cut into sixths and freeze

Notes

modified from King Arthur Banana Bread

Crepe

Ingredients

  • 2 large egg
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (melted)
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs and milk together
  2. Add the rest of the ingredients and whisk until smooth
  3. Refrigerate at least one hour
  4. Heat a 10 inch nonstick skillet over medium
  5. Lightly grease with butter, wipe off excess
  6. If the batter is too thick to pour, thin it out with either milk or melted butter
  7. Hold the pan and pour in 1/3 cup of batter
  8. Immediately swirl the batter to evenly coat the bottom in a paper thin layer
  9. Cook until the top look dry, should take around 20 seconds
  10. Grab one edge of the crepe and cook on the second side for 10 seconds, or until edges start to brown

Notes

Based on Kenji's crêpe recipe

Linzer Cookies

Ingredients

  • 226 gram butter
  • 339 gram ap flour
  • 113 gram powdered sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 egg yolk
  • 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon almond extract
  • 2 teaspoon (1 lemon) lemon zest
  • 12 oz seedless fruit jam (raspberry, apricot, etc)

Steps

  1. Combine the salt and flour in a bowl
  2. Cream the butter and powdered sugar together
  3. Mix egg yolk, vanilla extract, almond extract, and lemon zest into the creamed butter
  4. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet
  5. Tightly wrap the dough in plastic wrap
  6. Chill for at least two hours
  7. Remove the dough from the fridge
  8. Preheat the oven to 350
  9. Gently roll out on a floured surface to 1 cm thickness
  10. Cut the dough into 3 inch rounds
  11. Using a 1 inch cutter, remove the center from half of the rounds
  12. Reroll scraps and continue process until all usable cookies are shaped
  13. Bake the cookies for 14 minutes
  14. Move cookies to a cooling rack and allow to cool completely
  15. Spread two teaspoons of jam on each base cookie, top with the ring cookie
  16. Cookies can be enjoyed immediately, but improve with an overnight rest to allow absorption of jam moisture

New York Pizza Dough

Ingredients

  • 450 grams bread flour
  • 10 grams white sugar
  • 10 grams salt
  • 10 grams active dry yeast
  • 20 grams extra virgin olive oil
  • 280 grams water (warm)

Steps

  1. Combine dry ingredients in a KitchenAid mixer on low with a dough hook
  2. Add the wet ingredients and mix on low until combined
  3. Scrape the sides of the bowl and knead on high speed for 10 minutes, or until the dough becomes smooth to the touch
  4. Store the dough in an oiled bowl
  5. Refrigerate the dough at least overnight, best after two to three days.
  6. When using the dough, make sure to allow it to come up to room temperature before working, at least an hour or two

Notes

I like this recipe when I can cook on really high heat, like the grill

Pan Pizza Dough

Ingredients

  • 630 grams bread flour
  • 15 grams white sugar
  • 10 grams salt
  • 10 grams active dry yeast
  • 30 grams extra virgin olive oil
  • 420 grams water (warm)

Steps

  1. Combine dry ingredients in a food processor
  2. Pulse three to four times until combined
  3. Add wet ingredients and process for 15 seconds
  4. Process an additional 15 seconds
  5. Bring the dough together into a smooth ball
  6. Tightly cover the dough in an oiled metal bowl
  7. Let the dough rise in the fridge for 1-3 days
  8. Split the ball into thirds and store each portion in a quart container
  9. Use immediately, or freeze for up to two months

Notes

I like this receipe when cooking inside with the oven

It works fine on a vented pizza pan, better if that pan is on a pizza steel

Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 5 ounces ap flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon powder of baking
  • 1/4 teaspoon soda of baking
  • 1/2 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 large egg (separated)
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (melted)
  • ounces 6 buttermilk
  • ounces 4 sour cream

Steps

  1. Preheat a griddle to 325
  2. Preheat oven to "Keep warm" setting with a cooling rack on a sheet pan
  3. Combine dry ingredients in bowl
  4. In an imersion blender column, whip the white of an egg to stiff peaks
  5. In a second glass bowl, melt the butter
  6. Stir the buttermilk, egg yolk, and sour cream into the melted butter
  7. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the wet mix
  8. Gently combine the wet mix into the dry mix
  9. Dish pancakes out onto the griddle at desired size
  10. Once cooked through, keep warm in oven

Notes

Taken from Serious Eats.

The sour cream can be substituted 1 to 1 with more buttermilk, but the final texture won't be quite as fluffy

Pillows

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup splenda zero
  • 9 oz skim milk greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups ap flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon soda of baking
  • mixins (see step 1)

Steps

  1. The mixins will determine what kind of pillow you make. My favorite is 2 cups of frozen blueberries. We have also had success with 1/2 cup of mini chocolate chips. Anything you would use to flavor a muffin should work great
  2. Preheat oven to 400
  3. In a bowl, stir the flour, soda of baking, and salt together
  4. In a separate large bowl, whisk the splenda and eggs together until splenda is evenly distributed
  5. Whisk in the vanilla, applesauce, and greek yogurt
  6. Using a spatula, gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet mix. Do not overwork the batter.
  7. Gently fold the mixins into the batter
  8. Using an ice cream disher, drop 12 gobs of batter onto two foiled and greased sheet pans
  9. Bake until the pillows are just starting to turn golden (15 to 20 minutes, depending on the choice of mixins)
  10. Move the cooked pillows to a wire rack
  11. Once cooled, store in refrigerator for up to one week.
  12. Before serving, toast the pillow to reconstitute it.

Pizza Process

Ingredients

Steps

Notes

Making pizza at home involves a number of small steps that need to be executed perfectly.

First, I like to make a big batch of dough and freeze until needed. For a nice fluffy pie, I will use 1/2 a batch of Keni's Pan Pizza Dough. Not the 1/3rd from the recipe. Move the dough from the freezer to the fridge (still in the quart container) the day before you want to make a pie.

Two to three hours (depending on room temperature), move the fridge dough to the counter. Open the container and cover with plastic wrap.

One hour before you want to make a pizza, put a 1/2 inch pizza steel near the top of a gas oven. It should be just low enough so that a pie can slide in with clearance for the broiler. Set the temperature to 550. Put a sheet pan under the steel to catch any extra cornmeal.

While the stone is preheating, make a batch of Pizza Sauce, shred some low moisture full fat mozz, and put some pepperoni in a little dish. Having all the igredients ready makes the final steps work. If you aren't prepared, then the dough will stick to the peel.

Get a little dish of course cornmeal and a second little dish of AP Flour. Bring out your largest cutting board. Place a pizza peel on the board and top the peel with parchment paper. Flour the parchment paper. Flour a rolling pin.

Make sure the steel is hot enough to cook the pie. You want an infared thermometer to read at least 500 degrees on a couple surface points. Note that if you go above 600, then the dough will burn, so this recipe doesn't work on a grill.

Using the rolling pin, and reflouring as needed, work the dough into an even thickness round on the parchment. Once desired thickness is acheieved, sprinkle the dough with a generous coating of cornmeal. Remove the peel from under the dough. Flip the peel and put it on the cornmeal side of the dough. Use the parchment paper to quickly flip the peel and dough over, landing the cornmeal side of the dough on the peel.

Test moving the dough. If anything sticks (especially near the lip of the dough), then toss a heavy pinch of cornmeal under it. Note that when testing the dough's movement, all that should be required is a light touch. If you need to use too much force, then cornmeal is needed. Otherwise, the ingredients will slide all over the place.

Over the course of about 30 seconds, add a light amount of sauce to the dough. It should almost touch the edge of the round. Test the dough's ability to move and add cornmeal if needed. Sprinkle 1/4 pound of shreed mozzarella over the sauce. Test the dough's ability to move and add cornmeal if needed. Fill in sauce and cheese gaps with pepperoni. Do not overload on toppings. Test the dough's ability to move and add cornmeal if needed.

If the dough is mobile, then gently slide it in little flicks onto the pizza steel. Make sure to load as far back as possible to avoid the pizza overhanging the front of the steel. Turn the heat off on the oven and set the broiler to high.

Optionally set aside a couple small dipping dishes with extra sauce. These will be used for dunking the crust of each slice after eating.

The pizza will be fully cooked within 10 minutes, but go by look and smell. I often give the pie a turn or two while cooking, and switch off the broiler once the toppings reach desired doneness. The most important tell is that the bottom of the pie is taking on a nice peopard spotting appearance.

Set the pie on a cooling rack over the previously used parchment paper. Leave it here for a minute or two to allow steam to escape, locking in that crispy crust. While allowing steam to escape, sprinkle the pie with dried oregano and micrograte parm and pecorino over the top

Once ready to serve, use the peel to move the pizza back to the large cutting board. Use a sharp round pizza cutter to make the desired number of slices. With my 1/2 dough recipe, I like 6 big slices.

Previous Attempts

Note that this current list is the result of years of making pizzas in a gas oven. Every tool, ingredient, and step is essential. Leave anything out, and the entire pie will be subpar.

One major step that finalized this process was switching from parchment paper to cornmeal. Parchment paper makes getting the pie in the oven much easier. However, you need to pull it out before it lights on fire.

That little bit of time to pull the pie out and let heat escape results in a weaker rise and a less flavorful pie.

I wanted to try course semolina, but I couldn't find it anywhere around me. After trying cornmeal, I am certain all my favorite pizza joints were using cornmeal on their doughs.

You want to flour the dough as little as possible when shaping it on the peel. Otherwise it will develop a poor tasting thin layer of unhydrated flour that will make the pizza taste raw and bitter.

I have tried to make NY style super thin pizzas, but that will be a different write up. I like to roll for this method because it gives a better height across the entire pie.

Sloppy Fries

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 16 ounces manwich sauce
  • 16 ounces crinkle cut french fries (frozen)
  • 1 tablespoon fresh garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon fresh flat parsley (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon light olive oil
  • 1/4 cup house cheese blend
  • 1/8 cup ketchup

Steps

  1. Prepare the fries as instructed on the bag
  2. While the fries are cooking, brown the ground beef in a pan
  3. Once the beef is browned, add the manwich sauce to the pan. Cover and barely simmer.
  4. While the fries continue cooking, combine the olive oil, garlic, parsley, and salt and pepper to taste in a large bowl
  5. Immediately after the fries are finished cooking, coat them evenly with the oil mixture by tossing them in the bowl
  6. Plate the fries and cover with a quick zig zag of ketchup
  7. Scoop the sloppy joe over the ketchuped fries
  8. Sprinkle the cheese blend over the sloppy joe mix
  9. Give the dish another quick zig zag of ketchup

Sweet Nutty Granola

  • 200 grams cashews (chopped)
  • 400 grams smooth peanut butter

  • 250 grams maple syrup
  • 100 grams light brown sugar
  • 6 grams almond extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees
  2. In a large bowl, stir together the oats, crispy cereal, pepitas, cashews, cinnamon, and salt

Tim's Rice Krispy Treats

Ingredients

  • 16 oz marshmallow
  • 6.4 oz rice krispy cereal
  • 3.2 oz butter

Steps

  1. In a medium pot, melt the butter on the lowest heat setting
  2. Add the marshmallows to the pot
  3. Still on the lowest heat setting, stir the marshmallows until completely melted and mixed with the butter
  4. Take the pot off the heat, and quickly yet gently fold in the rice krispy cereal
  5. Immediately after the cereal is mixed, gently dump the mix into a greased 9 x 13 baking dish
  6. It is critical that you don't press the mix into the pan, but rather tap the mix gently to evently distribute
  7. Let the treats cool for 5 minutes, and then flip onto a greased cutting board
  8. After 30 minutes, the mix will have cooled enough for you to cut
  9. Cut the treats into 20 servings
  10. Once the treats are cool to the touch, they can be stored

Tim's Soft Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 113 grams (1 stick) butter
  • 30 grams (2 tablespoon) sugar
  • 90 grams (1/4 cup) light brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 3 grams (1 teaspoon) vanilla
  • 160 grams (1 1/8 cup) ap flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3 grams (1/2 teaspoon) soda of baking
  • 40 grams (1/4 cup) vanilla instant pudding mix
  • 130 grams (1/2 cup) chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325
  2. Mix flour, salt, pudding mix, and soda of baking in a small bowl
  3. Cream the light brown sugar and white sugar into the butter in a second, larger bowl with a silicone spatula
  4. Mix in egg and vanilla until air incorporates into the dough, making it lighter
  5. Barely mix the dry into the wet
  6. Gently fold in chocolate chips
  7. (Optional) Refrigerate the dough for at least 4 hours to improve flavor and texture
  8. Measure the dough and divide into 12-14 cookies (40-50g per cookie recommended)
  9. Evenly space the balls on a silicon lined half sheet pan
  10. Bake the cookies for 13 minutes, or until the bottoms are barely golden brown
  11. Quickly move the cookies from the sheet pan onto a cooling rack lined with parchment

Notes

The original version of this provided an underbacked doughy consistency, we no longer prefer that over the fully set cookie.

If upping the size to 90g per cookie, then up bake time to 18 minutes

Mods

Chocolate Orange

Replace 1/2 the choc chips with chopped chocolate orange candy

Add a teaspoon of orange extract to the dough

Peanut Butter Kiss

Substitute butter and chocolate chips for 160g smooth peanut butter and 90g shortening. Double the salt. Double the white sugar.

Make cookies that are half the normal size (~24g). Bake for 9 minutes. Press kisses into cookies immediately after leaving oven.

Do not thumb print the cookie dough balls before baking.

There is conflicting data from when I did the trials for the PB cookie. I prefer the mod as written, but a lot of people liked a 50g shortening + 50g butter split instead of 90g shortening.

Dark chocolate chunk cookies

At the mixin stage, chop the cholate bar into large pieces

Separate the flakes from the chunks

Mix the chunks in with the chocolate chips

Equally distribute the large chunks on the top of each dough ball

Pull the cookies at the regular time, but let them cool on the tray for 4 minutes before moving to a rack

  • 15 ounces tomato sauce (unseasoned)
  • 15 ounces pinto beans
  • 15 ounces black beans
  • 16 ounces kidney beans
  • 9 ounces white onion
  • 16 ounces red bell pepper
  • 16 ounces green bell pepper
  • 8 ounces celery
  • 10 ounces carrot
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 tablespoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon msg (accent)
  • 1 ounce william's original chili seasoning
  • 1 splash cooking oil (peanut)
  • 1 bag corn chip scoops (optional)
  • 1 container plain greek yogurt (optional)

Steps

  1. Dice the carrots, sweet peppers, celery, and white onion. I dice each chunk of carrot and pepper to twice the volume of the celery and onion
  2. Add the diced aromatics to a large cold pot
  3. Splash the veggies with the cooking oil
  4. Sprinkle the salt, ground black pepper, garlic powder, and MSG into the pot
  5. Give everything a stir and bring the heat up to sweat the vegetables
  6. While sweating, remove the soy chorizo from its packaging and have at the ready
  7. Once a carrot is about half way to the doneness you prefer, stir in the soy chorizo. I look for the carrot to be just barely starting to soften to a fork
  8. Open and rinse the pinto, black, and kidney beans. Skipping the bean rinse will result in an unpleasant earthy flavor in the final prepped chili
  9. After the soy chorizo is brought to temperature and the pot begins sizzling again, stir in the rinsed beans
  10. Stir in the tomato sauce, crushed tomatoes, and diced tomatoes
  11. Stir in the chili seasoning
  12. Simmer for at least ten minutes before serving
  13. Best results will be obtained by allowing the chili to cool and then storing in the fridge at least overnight
  14. This chili goes great with a side of corn chips and a dollop of plain greek yogurt
